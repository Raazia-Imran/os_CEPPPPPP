**Plain-language terms:** A *hit* is one search-result record, not a paper read. *Deduplication* links the same paper found more than once. *Screening* decides relevance from titles/abstracts, then full text. *Snowballing* checks references of included papers and later papers that cite them; it is a separate discovery route after initial searches. *PRISMA* is the diagram showing how many records moved through each stage. *Data extraction* means taking specific facts and measurements from the chosen papers into a table, with a page or figure so they can be verified.

**Do not aim for 800 or 900 hits.** The guideline values reproducible searching and 15–20 eligible primary studies at final inclusion. Report the real hits even if much fewer or greater. It is normal for many first-stage hits to be irrelevant. Group A's searches do not count as Group B's four executed searches.
