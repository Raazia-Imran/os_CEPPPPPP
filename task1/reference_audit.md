## Findings that affect use of the references

1. **One definite field error was fixed:** the issuing institutions in `kitchenham`. All 18 works or pages have corroborating source records; none appeared to be an invented citation.
2. **Three direct-page limits remain:** the cited ACM help page, the Petersen publisher-hosted record, and the ParvaGPU DOI/publisher page could not be opened directly in this check. Their identities have other official, institutional, or indexed corroboration as described above. A blocked page is not evidence that a work is false.
3. **The IEEE guide is old:** it verifies the cited 2018 document, not acceptance of every planned query in the 2026 interface. The ACM and IEEE exact field behavior requires an actual logged database pilot. Likewise, these checks do not establish NED access rights.
4. **Source roles matter:** Kitchenham/Petersen/PRISMA/PRISMA-S are methodology; NED and database help pages support resource and interface descriptions; NVIDIA supports vendor-defined mechanism terminology; four proceedings papers are candidate seeds; Yu et al. is contextual secondary literature. None of these entries represents a completed search or a final included-study decision.

The bibliography preserves URLs and document identities. The manuscript compiles with BibTeX after the correction. For later publication-oriented formatting, DOI notes could be moved into dedicated `doi` fields without changing their verified values.
