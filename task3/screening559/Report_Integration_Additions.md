## Taxonomy and comparison additions

LithOS(S021) adds transparent driver-level interposition, TPC-level allocation, atomization and dynamic right-sizing. Its inference-only and mixed training/inference experiments must stay separate. Comparisons with MIG and other systems have unequal supported tenant sets and partition configurations, limiting direct numerical ranking.

Abacus(S022) adds deterministic operator-overlap control and latency prediction across separate inference services. Record prediction/profiling cost and the distinction between deadline-dropped queries, latency populations and QoS-violation counts. Retain the reported hardware-description inconsistency rather than silently normalizing it.

Miriam(S023) adds elastic-kernel transformation and mixed-criticality scheduling on edge devices. Closed-loop and open-loop outcomes differ; its introductory latency summary is not a bound for every evaluated workload. Preserve the source's platform-label inconsistency and distinguish conference year from index/publication-date fields.

SGDRC(S024) adds software control of compute and VRAM-bandwidth contention. Its shared CUDA context explicitly lacks DNN GPU-runtime fault isolation. The reimplemented baseline, disabled dynamic batching, device-specific support and adverse raw-throughput cases must accompany the reported goodput benefits.

Pantheon(S025) adds chunk-level preemption with early-exit model variants for real-time inference. It improves deadline behavior while trading model accuracy, memory and scheduler overhead. Deadline-adjusted relative accuracy counts late jobs as zero and must not be compared directly with ordinary classification accuracy. Similar utilization to RTm-DL does not establish universal utilization superiority.

Oneiros(S026) adds parameter-memory remapping and asynchronous CPU/GPU transfer for multiple resident LLM services under temporal and spatial sharing. Its MPS-like spatial comparison reports65.5%lowerP99TBT,20.7%lowerP99TTFT and6.6%higher token throughput on the stated C1 workload. These are author-reported averages within one system and workload context. Preserve the inconsistent isolation caption, platform labels and the latency cost of excessive remapping. Do not infer a new security/fault-isolation guarantee from memory-capacity management.

## RQ synthesis and engineering recommendation

The additions strengthen evidence for conditional choices: deterministic overlap and fine-grained preemption can improve latency predictability, while memory management can relieve capacity pressure. Their costs differ across profiling, model modification, kernel/driver compatibility, transfer bandwidth and isolation boundaries. These mechanisms do not establish a universal winner over time-slicing, MPS, MIG or API remoting.
