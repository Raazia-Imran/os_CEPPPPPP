## Final integration sequence

1. Finish the current source-review cycle and regenerate every dependent register.
2. Rebuild the report, master and heading checklist from the same decisions.
3. Check source anchors, references, count reconciliation, QA denominators and report layout. Retain adverse findings and actual unknowns.
4. Apply teammate corrections, repeat affected checks, and record the final commit.
5. Submit the agreed PDF plus required supporting materials. Use the current workbook and logs, not a mixture of frozen and current counts.

The report is a first draft while selection remains open. Meeting the study minimum does not close the remaining eligibility queue. A deadline does not justify excluding records or removing limitations. A later eligible study changes the working corpus and dependent synthesis; an exclusion changes the eligibility log and flow accounting. Neither requires rewriting the frozen snapshot.
