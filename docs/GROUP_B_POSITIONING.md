# Group B's independent analysis plan

The assigned Topic 23 and the submitted protocol already require all four families: time-slicing, MPS, MIG and API remoting. Group B will retain the submitted RQs and eligibility rules.

For its eventual report, Group B will put **per-tenant latency predictability and interference** at the center of the engineering decision. In each eligible paper, record the actual latency quantile, request load, tenant count, workload mix, GPU generation, sharing settings and comparator. Distinguish a direct isolation experiment from an author statement or vendor specification. Compare utilization or density improvements only alongside the latency/isolation condition that produced them. Report where p95/p99 latency, faults, or interference were not measured, rather than assuming a favorable outcome.

This emphasis fits the submitted RQ2–RQ5 and can yield original tables: (1) mechanism and isolation boundary, (2) within-study utilization versus tail latency under matching loads, and (3) conditional selection by SLO and workload mix. It is a **planned analysis**, not a proven research gap or a conclusion. Do not claim novelty over existing surveys until current reviews and all four databases have been checked.

Group A's shared metadata can point Group B toward a paper; it cannot supply Group B's vote, quality score, numerical extraction, PRISMA count or drafted interpretation. Credit the cited primary paper, preserve the shared-lead origin in the log and independently read/check the paper. Papers may overlap while the actual selection and synthesis remain separately documented.
